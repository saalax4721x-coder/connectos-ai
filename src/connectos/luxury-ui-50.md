## Quality bar
ConnectOS must not resemble a generic AI dashboard. It should feel like a premium intelligence platform: editorial, cinematic, restrained, spacious, sophisticated, fast, and intentional. Motion should communicate hierarchy and state rather than exist as decoration. Avoid excessive gradients, neon AI aesthetics, noisy cards, template-like dashboards, and childish animations.
