# ConnectOS AI — Part 1 Foundation

## Product mission
ConnectOS is a personalized AI business operating system for ambitious people. It helps users define a goal, understand their situation, choose a strategy, execute a four-week plan, discover opportunities, and track progress.

## Core experience
1. Authentication and account creation.
2. AI onboarding asks role, primary goal, industries, location, experience, skills, and optional business/career context.
3. A personalization profile drives the home dashboard, recommendations, strategy, opportunities, and feed.
4. Home is a clean command center with current goal, progress, AI briefing, today's top three moves, journey status, and recommendations.
5. Strategy provides low-risk, medium-risk, and high-growth pathways with timelines, actions, risks, and reasons for fit. Financial guidance is educational and never guarantees returns.
6. Career Builder helps users without a career identify suitable paths, skills, learning plans, and 30/90-day milestones.
7. Business Builder helps users identify problems, validate ideas, choose business models, and follow a four-week launch roadmap.
8. Journey Planner creates weekly milestones and lets users mark tasks complete and track progress.
9. Progress visuals use premium, motivating illustrations/animations such as climbing a mountain or building toward a goal. The tone is ambitious and cinematic, never childish.

## Navigation
Bottom navigation: Home, Strategy, Opportunities, Network, Circles. Part 2 will connect the Network, Circles, search, profiles, and intelligence systems.

Left sidebar: Profile, Search, Notifications, Settings, Saved Items, Subscription, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Delete Account.

## Themes
Support five premium themes: Executive Black, Royal Blue, Emerald Elite, Platinum White, Midnight Gold. Theme selection changes surfaces, accents, navigation, buttons, and dashboard styling while preserving accessibility.

## Home requirements
The home page must be calm and uncluttered. It should answer immediately: What is my goal? How close am I? What should I do today? What does AI recommend?

Sections: greeting, current goal and percentage, AI Executive Briefing, Today's Top 3 Moves (money/growth/network), four-week journey, recommended actions, and personalized intelligence feed placeholder.

## AI behavior
The assistant should transform user context into actionable recommendations rather than generic lists. Recommendations should explain why they fit the user and should be measurable where possible.

## Notifications
Support valuable notifications for AI recommendations, opportunities, network activity, messages, and progress. Include user controls to prevent notification overload.

## Settings and account
Support account recovery, profile settings, theme settings, notification settings, privacy controls, language selection, and account deletion flow.

## Architecture rule
This file is the product foundation specification. Future implementation must preserve the existing features and extend the system rather than replacing it. Part 2 adds intelligence, networking, messaging, professional profiles, company intelligence, search, circles, personalized feeds, and leaderboards. Part 3 adds data integrations, subscriptions, security, analytics, legal, and launch readiness.
